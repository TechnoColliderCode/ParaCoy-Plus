=========================================================
      PAREKOY MUSIC BOT - COMPLETE COMMAND REFERENCE
=========================================================

For a fun listening experience, follow the command guides below. All commands are just typed in the chat (no slash needed).

---------------------------------------------------------
1. PLAYING A SONG (STREAMING)
---------------------------------------------------------

p, play <search term or URL>
   - Search and play from YouTube.
   - Example: p Eraserheads Ang Huling El Bimbo
   - Benefit: Automatic skipping if there's an error or video not available.

u <URL>
   - Play from Facebook, TikTok, Instagram, or Twitter/X.
   - Example: u https://fb.watch/reels/12345/
   - Benefit: You can listen to trending reels directly on TeamTalk.

il, bilibili <search or URL>
   - Play from Bilibili.tv.
   - Example: il anime ost english
   - Benefit: Best for anime and dubbed series content.

ra, amradio <search>
   - Search AM stations in the Philippines (e.g., DZMM, DZRH).
   - Example: ra 630 am

rf, fmradio <search>
   - Search FM stations (e.g., Love Radio, Moro FM).

n (Next) / b (Back)
   - Use this while searching to switch to another result (Channel Surfing).

---------------------------------------------------------
2. WATTPAD DOWNLOADER (PRO VERSION)
---------------------------------------------------------

wp
   - Wattpad story downloader. 
   - Step 1: Type 'wp'.
   - Step 2: Paste the story link.
   - Step 3: Choose 'y' to include pictures, 'n' for text only.
   - Benefit: Turns it into a professional EPUB book with cover and images.

---------------------------------------------------------
3. PLAYBACK CONTROLS
---------------------------------------------------------

s (Stop) - Stop the music.
ss (Stop All) - Stop everything and clear the queue.
x (Pause/Resume) - Pause or resume the song.
v <0-100> - Increase or decrease the volume (Example: v 80).
+ / - <seconds> - Fast forward or Rewind (Example: +30 or -10).
j <seconds> - Jump to a specific time (Example: j 120 for 2:00 mins).
e <rate> - Speed up/down (Example: e 1.5 for faster).
m <2, 3, 4> - Playback Mode: 2=Once, 3=Repeat One, 4=Repeat All.
sh (Shuffle) - Shuffle the songs in the queue.

---------------------------------------------------------
4. QUEUE & FAVORITES
---------------------------------------------------------

q <search> - Add to queue without stopping the current song.
pq [index] - Play from the current queue or specified index.
fe (Fav Add) - Save the current song to your Favorites list.
fl (Fav List) - Show the list of your favorite songs.
pf <number> - Play a song from the Favorites list (Example: pf 1).

---------------------------------------------------------
5. FILE SHARING (DOWNLOADS)
---------------------------------------------------------

dl (Download) - Download the track as MP3 and upload it to the channel.
dlv (Video) - Download the full video (MP4) and upload it to the channel.
* Note: The server has an automatic cleanup of files after 5-15 mins.

---------------------------------------------------------
6. SYSTEM & MAINTENANCE
---------------------------------------------------------

rs (Remote Restart) - Restart the bot connection if it lags.
resette - Reset the TeamTalk SDK license when it becomes "Trial Expired".
qq (Quit) - Remotely kill the bot application.
a (About) / h (Help) - Info and list of commands.

---------------------------------------------------------
7. ADMINISTRATIVE CONTROL
---------------------------------------------------------

cn <nick>        - Modify the bot's display nickname.
sn               - Persist the current nickname to server configuration.
cg command. to change bot's gender. m for man. f for woman. n for newtral.
adminadd <user>  - Grant administrative privileges to a specific user.
                   Example: adminadd Owen
admindel <user>  - Revoke administrative privileges from a specific user.
                   Example: admindel Owen
adminlist        - Display a comprehensive list of authorized administrators.
lock             - Toggle bot restriction (Restricts access to admins only).
status <message> - Update the bot's broadcast status message.
